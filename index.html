Sneak Next Door

A fan-made VR stealth game (not an official game). The neighbor’s mansion has three floors, including the basement. Your mission: 1) find the brass key upstairs in the far bedroom, 2) unlock the vault in the back-right of the basement and steal the glowing gem, 3) escape out the front door. He chases you the moment he sees you, but he never leaves his house. Crouch and stay still to hide from him. Turn your sound up.

Left controller only: Stick = move · Grip + stick left/right = snap turn · Stick click = sprint (loud) · X = crouch · Y = flashlight · Trigger = grab / throw (throw things to lure him)
